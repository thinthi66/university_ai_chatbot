# International Student Accommodation

The University of Mauritius states that it is **non-residential**. International students therefore need to make their own accommodation arrangements.

UoM's International Affairs information indicates that a list of accommodation providers is available.

Students should use current official UoM guidance before paying deposits or making arrangements.

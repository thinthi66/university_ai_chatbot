# International Student Visa and Residence Permit

UoM states that students should **not travel to Mauritius without a provisional student permit** issued by the Passport and Immigration Office.

UoM's current visa guidance states that:
- Passport validity should cover the programme duration.
- A provisional visa/permit is initially granted and the student residence-permit process follows.
- Student Entry/Residence Permit applications are submitted through the relevant national electronic licensing system using a unique student enrolment key.
- Documents include passport evidence, photo, offer/acceptance documents, character certificate, financial evidence, sponsorship evidence where relevant, proof of accommodation and required medical reports.
- The page specifies detailed medical requirements and document validity conditions.

The visa page should be used as the authoritative current checklist because immigration requirements can change.

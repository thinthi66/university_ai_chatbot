# Students' Union

The Students' Union is the official organisation of University of Mauritius students and is the centre of student non-academic activities.

Its two main functions are:
1. To represent students in matters concerning them.
2. To promote and provide for student welfare and social, cultural and educational activities.

All full-time and part-time enrolled students are members.

**Office:** Students' Union Office, 1st Floor Student Center, University of Mauritius, Réduit  
**Telephone:** 4037400 Ext. 4016  
**Email:** presidentsu@uom.ac.mu

The Union may recognise and finance student societies covering recreational activities, academic subjects and topics of concern to students.

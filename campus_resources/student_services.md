# Student Services and Useful Contacts

Official UoM student materials identify services including:
- Admissions and Student Records Office
- Public Relations Office
- Students' Union
- First Aid Posts
- Sports Unit
- Library
- IT Support / ITS
- MIS support

Examples from official UoM materials:
- Admissions: 403 7400; admission@uom.ac.mu
- Public Relations: 403 7400 Ext. 5215; pro@uom.ac.mu
- First Aid: 403 7699 / 403 7400 Ext. 5325
- Sports Unit: 403 7400 Ext. 5330/5335 or 4676576; sportsunit@uom.ac.mu
- IT Support: helpdesk@uom.ac.mu
- MIS: mishelpdesk@uom.ac.mu

Contact extensions can vary between official faculty materials, so users should be given the source and current central contact where possible.

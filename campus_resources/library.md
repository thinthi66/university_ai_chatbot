# University Library

## Opening hours
### Term time
- Weekdays: **08:00 - 17:00**
- Saturday: **08:00 - 13:00**

### Vacation
- Weekdays: **08:00 - 16:00**
- Saturday: **08:00 - 12:00**

## Loan periods
- General collection: **14 days**, renewable once at the counter if there is no reservation
- Law books: **7 days**, renewable for another 7 days if there is no reservation
- Library Science books: **7 days**; consultation/reference conditions apply
- Reserve Section: **overnight or weekend loan**; during opening hours, consultation is in the library
- Academic, senior administrative and research staff and visiting lecturers: loan/renewal period listed as **1 month**

The official Library page also publishes overdue fines and external-user access information. These amounts should be rechecked before being quoted.

# International Foundation Programmes

For international applicants, UoM's 2026/2027 programme page lists:
- **F001SC - Science Stream - 1 Year Full Time**
- **F001SH - Social Sciences Stream - 1 Year Full Time**

These foundation streams are marked as applicable to international applicants only.

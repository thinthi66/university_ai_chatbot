# Application Process

## Mauritian applicants
The University of Mauritius uses an online application system. The official system provides programme-specific application openings and deadlines.

**Admissions contact**
- Telephone: (230) 403-7400
- Email: admission@uom.ac.mu

## International applicants
The international application area provides information on how to apply, general and specific entry requirements, fees, accommodation, health, visa and residence permits.

For the August 2026 international undergraduate/foundation and postgraduate intake, UoM listed:
- Apply online by: **10 April 2026**
- Application-fee payment deadline: **10 April 2026**

Postgraduate research applications are handled separately and the online application system states that postgraduate research applications are open throughout the year.

## Chatbot guidance
Always check the current official application page before stating that an intake is open or closed because deadlines change by intake and programme.

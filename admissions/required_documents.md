# Required Documents

Document requirements vary by applicant type and programme. International applicants should consult the current application and visa pages.

For student entry/residence permit processing, UoM lists documents including:
- Passport bio-data pages
- Recent passport-size digital photograph
- Letter of Offer
- Letter of Acceptance from the student
- Police/Character Certificate meeting stated validity requirements
- Evidence of sufficient funds
- Sponsorship letter and sponsor identity evidence where applicable
- Proof of accommodation in Mauritius
- Medical reports required by the immigration process

Do not present this list as the complete academic-admission document list for every programme; it is specifically relevant to the international student permit process.

# Undergraduate and Postgraduate Regulations 2026/2027

UoM labels the 2026/2027 Undergraduate/Postgraduate Regulations as **Under Review** and warns that they may be subject to change.

The regulations cover, among other areas:
1. Selection and admission to taught programmes
2. Programme structure and credit system
3. Student academic progress and complaints
4. Fees and other charges
5. Admission of international students
6. Financial assistance, scholarships, prizes and medals
7. Students' Union
8. Membership of University committees
9. Academic dress

The fees section includes late module registration/de-registration, module exemption, re-examination, re-registration, remarking/review, appeals, transcripts/certificates, ID/password-related charges and graduation-related fees.

The international-student section includes application procedures, entry requirements, fees, Student Welfare Office, visa/residence permit and accommodation.

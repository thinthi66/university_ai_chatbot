# Registration and Examinations

UoM's regulations include provisions for late module registration/de-registration, module exemption, re-examination, re-registration, remarking/review of examination scripts or dissertations, and appeals.

Because procedures and charges can change, the chatbot should answer from the current regulation section and calendar relevant to the student's academic year and programme. It should not infer a deadline from an earlier academic year.

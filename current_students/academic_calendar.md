# Academic Calendar 2026/2027

UoM publishes four Calendar of Major Activities documents for 2026/2027:
- LCCS Semester 1
- LCCS Semester 2
- Non-LCCS Semester 1
- Non-LCCS Semester 2

The calendar should be treated as programme/credit-system specific. The chatbot must determine whether the student's programme follows LCCS or Non-LCCS before presenting detailed dates.

The official LCCS Semester 2 calendar states:
- Semester 2 Special Retake Examinations: **26 July 2027 to 6 August 2027**
- Students' Union Elections: **31 August 2026 to 4 September 2026**

Always use the appropriate official calendar document for exact lecture, test, examination, project/dissertation and results-processing dates.

# Scholarships, Financial Assistance and Student Support

The UoM regulations contain a dedicated section on **Financial Assistance to Students and Scholarships/Prizes**, including:
- Assistance to needy students
- Scholarships
- Prizes and medals

The regulations also identify student welfare/facilities including the Students' Union, First Aid, Library, Sports and Games, food outlets, banks, parking, Student Counselling Unit, photocopy/printing and other facilities.

For a specific scholarship, the chatbot should retrieve the current scholarship notice or official UoM scholarship information rather than assume eligibility.

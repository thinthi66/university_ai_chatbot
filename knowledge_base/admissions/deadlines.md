# Application Deadlines — University of Mauritius

**Important:** Deadlines change every intake. Always verify on the official website.

## August 2026 Intake (Academic Year 2026/2027)

### Mauritian Applicants
- Online application and payment of application fee: Check the latest deadline on the Admissions page (previous re-advertisement closed around mid-May 2026 for some programmes).
- Always check: https://www.uom.ac.mu/index.php/undergraduate-programmes-on-offer-mauritian-aug2026

### International Applicants
- Previous deadline for August 2026 intake was extended to **10 April 2026**.
- Online application: https://apply.uom.ac.mu/internationalapp

## General Advice

- Application windows usually open several months before the start of the academic year.
- Postgraduate research programmes may accept applications throughout the year.
- Always confirm the exact deadline on the official UoM Admissions pages or by emailing admission@uom.ac.mu / iao@uom.ac.mu.

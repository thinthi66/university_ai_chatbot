# General Entry Requirements — University of Mauritius

**Source:** University Regulations (Chapter 1 & Chapter 14) and Admissions pages  
**Academic Year:** 2026/2027

## Undergraduate Degree Programmes

Typical minimum requirements include:

- A pass in English Language at Cambridge School Certificate / GCE ‘O’ Level or equivalent
**AND**
- Credit in three other subjects at Cambridge School Certificate / GCE ‘O’ Level  
**OR** equivalent qualifications acceptable to the University.

Specific programmes have additional requirements (see Individual Programme Entry Requirements).

## Postgraduate Degree Programmes

Successful completion of an undergraduate degree with:

- At least a Second Class or 50%, whichever is applicable, **or**
- A GPA not less than 2.5 out of 4 (or equivalent) from a recognised higher education institution

**OR** alternative qualifications acceptable to the University of Mauritius.

## English Language Requirement

Students who do not possess a recognised qualification in English must provide evidence of proficiency (e.g. TOEFL, IELTS or equivalent).

## International Students

International students must satisfy the **same minimum entry requirements** as local students, plus any programme-specific requirements.

## Notes

- Mature students and special cases are covered under University Regulations.
- Always check the specific programme requirements because many programmes have higher or additional criteria.

# Application Process — University of Mauritius

**Source:** https://www.uom.ac.mu/index.php/application-process  
**Last checked:** September 2026

## How to Apply

Applications to the University of Mauritius are submitted **online only**.

### Required Documents (to be uploaded)

- Copies of transcripts and certificates of previous qualifications (Grade 10/12, Degree Certificate, Birth Certificate, etc.)
- Originals must be provided later on the day of registration
- English language proficiency evidence (if the applicant does not have a recognised English qualification) — e.g. TOEFL, IELTS or equivalent
- Any other supporting documents
- Proof of payment of the Application Fee (with Application Number)

**Important:** Incomplete applications will not be considered.

## Offer of Seat

- Offers of seats are normally made approximately one month after the application deadline.
- Candidates may receive more than one offer.

## Acceptance of Seat

- Acceptance of an offer must be sent within **10 days** from the date the offer was made.
- Candidates must clearly state which programme they are accepting.
- Tuition fees must be paid within 10 days of the offer.
- Proof of payment should be sent to: **studyatuom@uom.ac.mu**

## Refund Policy (Visa-related)

- If a student does not obtain a visa from the Passport and Immigration Office, the total fees paid (excluding the application fee) will be refunded.
- For other withdrawals, the University Refund Policy (Section 13.4 of the Regulations) applies.

# Postgraduate Programmes — University of Mauritius

**Source:** Official UoM programme pages and Faculty listings  
**Note:** Availability changes by intake. Always check the official website.

## Examples of Postgraduate Programmes

### Faculty of Law and Management
- Master of Business Administration (MBA)
- MBA with specialisation in Human Resource Management
- MBA with specialisation in Marketing
- MSc International Business Management
- MSc Tourism and Hospitality Management

### Other Faculties
Postgraduate programmes (taught and research) are offered across:
- Faculty of Agriculture
- Faculty of Engineering
- Faculty of Information, Communication and Digital Technologies
- Faculty of Science
- Faculty of Social Sciences and Humanities
- Faculty of Medicine and Health Sciences
- Centre for Innovative and Lifelong Learning (CILL)

## Application Notes

- Taught postgraduate programmes have specific application windows.
- Postgraduate programmes by research may accept applications throughout the year.
- International applicants should use the international application portal.

**Recommended action for users:**  
Visit the official UoM website or contact admission@uom.ac.mu / iao@uom.ac.mu for the current list of postgraduate programmes open for application.

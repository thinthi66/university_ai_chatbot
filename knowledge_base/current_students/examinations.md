# Examinations — University of Mauritius

**Source:** University Regulations (Chapters related to Assessment and Examinations)

## Key Rules

- Only registered students who have properly registered for the modules on the Online Module Registration System are admitted to examinations.
- Examination timetables are published by the University.
- Students must check the official timetable and seating lists.

## Continuous Assessment + Examination

Most modules combine continuous assessment and a final written examination.  
An overall pass mark (commonly 40% combined) is required, subject to Faculty rules.

## Review of Scripts / Results

Students may apply for review of examination scripts within a limited number of working days after results are released (normally via the Student e-Review System). A fee is charged per module.

## Special Cases

- Absence due to illness requires proper medical documentation.
- Students on the debtors list may be barred from results or re-registration.

**For exact procedures and forms:** Consult the current University Regulations and the Examinations Office / Faculty Registry.

# Module Registration — University of Mauritius

**Source:** University Regulations Chapter 3 (Registration and Conduct of Students)

## Key Points

- Module registration is done **online** through the Online Module Registration System (OMRS).
- Registration normally opens **two weeks before** the start of the semester and remains open until the end of Week 3.
- Students are responsible for registering for the correct modules.

## Important Deadlines (typical pattern)

- Normal registration period: from 2 weeks before semester start until end of Week 3
- Late module registration / de-registration: usually allowed until the end of Week 7 (with penalty fee)
- Exact dates are published in the Academic Calendar each year

## Rules

- Students on the list of debtors cannot register for modules until fees are cleared.
- Failure to register for modules means the student will not be admitted to the examinations for those modules.
- Changes or withdrawals after the normal period attract an administrative/penalty fee.

# Library — University of Mauritius

**Source:** University Regulations Chapter 10 (Library)

## Mission

The University of Mauritius Library provides bibliographic support, services and facilities to meet the information needs of registered students, academic and technical staff.

## Membership

- Free for registered students and staff of the University.
- External users may apply for reference access (fees apply).

## Services

- Print collections
- Electronic resources / e-library (login credentials required)
- Reading and study facilities

## Important Rules

- Students must return all borrowed items before the end of their programme to obtain library clearance (required for graduation).
- External users pay fees according to the period of access (daily, weekly, monthly, semester, year).

**Contact:** Library counter / registry (check current telephone numbers on the UoM website).

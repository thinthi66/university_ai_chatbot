# Application for International Students — University of Mauritius

**Official portal:** https://apply.uom.ac.mu/internationalapp  
**Source:** University Regulations Chapter 14 and International Students pages

## How to Apply

1. Fill the online application form at the international portal.
2. Pay the non-refundable Application Fee of **USD 50**.
3. Send proof of payment + Application Number + full name + scanned documents to the email indicated on the portal (commonly intstudpaylt@uom.ac.mu).

## Required Documents (typical)

- Birth certificate
- Educational certificates and transcripts
- Professional certificates (if any)
- Passport copy
- Proof of English proficiency (if required)
- Any other documents requested on the portal

## Entry Requirements

International students must meet the same general and programme-specific entry requirements as local students.

## After Offer

- Accept the offer within the stated deadline.
- Pay the required fees.
- The University assists with the student visa / residence permit process (see visa_and_immigration.md).

**Contact:** iao@uom.ac.mu / admission@uom.ac.mu

# Visa and Residence Permit — International Students

**Source:** https://www.uom.ac.mu/index.php/iaointernational-students/visa  
**University Regulations Chapter 14**

## Important Rule

Students should **not** travel to Mauritius without a student entry permit issued by the Passport and Immigration Office. Doing so may result in deportation.

## How the Process Works

The University of Mauritius submits the visa / residence permit application on behalf of students who have been offered admission.

## Documents Commonly Required

- “Application form to Enter Mauritius” (filled and signed)
- Two recent passport-size photographs
- Photocopy of the first five pages of the passport (including photo and issue date)
- Original bank statement showing adequate funds (often evidence of at least MUR 150,000)
- Letter of sponsorship + copy of sponsor’s ID/passport (if applicable)
- Medical certificate (not suffering from contagious/infectious diseases) + HIV, Hepatitis B tests and Chest X-Ray
- Proof of accommodation in Mauritius
- Letter of acceptance from the student
- Police Character Certificate (less than 6 months old)

Documents should be submitted well in advance (ideally 2–3 months before arrival).

## After Arrival

Students must complete registration formalities at the Admissions and Student Records Office and may need to undergo additional medical tests in Mauritius for the full residence permit.

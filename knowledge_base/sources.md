## Notes for Maintainers

- University information (deadlines, fees, programmes) changes regularly. Always re-check the official website before major updates.
- Prefer PDF regulations and official admissions pages over third-party sites.
- When information is incomplete or outdated, the chatbot should say so and direct the user to the relevant office.

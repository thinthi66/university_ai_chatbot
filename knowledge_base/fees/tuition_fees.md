# Tuition Fees — University of Mauritius

**Official source:** https://www.uom.ac.mu/fees  
**Regulations:** Chapter 13 & Chapter 14 of University Regulations 2026/2027

## Important Notes

- Fees differ for Mauritian and International students.
- Fees also differ by programme type (Science & Technology / lab-based vs non-lab-based, flagship programmes, etc.).
- Exact figures change and must be verified on the official fees page.

## International Students (indicative structure from Regulations)

Tuition is often calculated on a per-credit basis. Examples of rates mentioned in regulations (subject to change):

| Category | Approximate rate guidance |
|----------|---------------------------|
| Flagship / high-demand programmes | Higher rate |
| Science & Technology (lab-based) | Medium-high rate |
| Non-Science & Technology | Lower rate |
| Distance / DEOL mode | Different rate |

General Fees (Registration, Library, Examination, Sports, Digital Learning Materials, Network Services) also apply.

International students may pay in Mauritian Rupees or equivalent foreign currency at the prevailing exchange rate.

## Mauritian Students

Many undergraduate programmes for Mauritian students have different (often lower or subsidised) fee structures. Some programmes are non-tuition-fee paying under certain conditions.
